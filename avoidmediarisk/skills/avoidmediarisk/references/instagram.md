# Instagram Risk Control & Recommendation Eligibility Rules

> Last Verified: 2026-11-24

## 1. "Shadowban" Reality: Recommendation Eligibility & Account Status

- **Recommendation Eligibility**: Rather than deleting posts or hiding them from existing followers, Instagram blocks non-compliant accounts/posts from **non-follower discovery surfaces** (Explore, Reels tab, Search, Suggested Accounts).
- **Account Status Diagnostic (`Profile → Menu / Settings → Account Status`)**:
  - Available for Creator and Business accounts.
  - Explicitly displays whether the account is eligible for recommendation to non-followers, which specific posts/Reels/profile elements (bio, avatar) violate Recommendations Guidelines, and provides a **Request a Review** button.

## 2. Reels Watermark & Originality Penalties

- **Third-Party Watermark Suppression**: Reels containing visible logos or watermarks from TikTok, CapCut, or competing editing apps are systematically excluded from Explore and Reels discovery feeds. Always export clean, unbranded video files.
- **Aggregator / Unoriginal Content**: Accounts that repeatedly repost or aggregate photos, carousels, or Reels without adding meaningful original transformation face systematic reach caps.

## 3. AI-Generated Profile vs. Post-Level AI Rules

- **"AI-Generated Profile" Label**:
  - Enforced at the **profile level** for accounts whose primary persona, avatar, or featured "person" is generated or substantially created with generative AI.
  - Undisclosed synthetic personas detected by Instagram or reported by users lose **recommendation eligibility** on Explore and Reels. Toggling the label on carries no algorithmic penalty.
  - **Exemption**: Human creators using AI only to edit photos, polish captions, or add minor graphics do **not** need the profile-level AI label.

## 4. Engagement Bait & ManyChat / DM Automation Compliance

- **Engagement Bait Suppression**: Low-effort posts or captions designed purely to farm comments/likes (e.g., hollow *"Comment X to get Y"* loops without genuine content substance) are downranked in favor of **Shares (Sends)** and **Watch Time/Retention**.
- **DM Automation Rules (ManyChat / Meta-Approved API Partners)**:
  - **Official API Only**: Never use unauthorized third-party growth bots, mass-follow/unfollow scripts, or engagement pods. High hourly rates of likes, follows, or identical comments trigger safety containment blocks.
  - **User Opt-In Mandatory**: Automated DMs may **only** be sent after a qualifying user-initiated interaction (commenting a keyword on a Reel/post, replying to a Story, or sending a DM). Cold-messaging or unsolicited broadcasts are prohibited.
  - **24-Hour Messaging Window**: Automated follow-ups must occur within **24 hours** of the user's last interaction.
  - **Avoid Identical Spam Replies**: Rotate/vary public comment replies to keyword triggers so Meta's anti-spam classifiers don't flag repetitive bot-like responses.

## 5. Pre-Publish & Triage Checklist

1. Check **Account Status** in settings first if non-follower reach suddenly drops; remove or appeal flagged posts/bio items.
2. Verify Reels have **zero** TikTok/CapCut/third-party watermarks.
3. If running a synthetic/AI persona account, ensure the **AI-Generated Profile** label is enabled.
4. For comment-to-DM workflows, use a Meta-approved API tool (e.g., ManyChat), respect the 24-hour opt-in window, vary public reply text, and ensure the Reel itself delivers strong retention/share value.
5. Avoid banned or spam-flooded hashtags and disconnect any unauthorized automation apps.
