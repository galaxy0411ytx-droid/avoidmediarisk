# YouTube Risk Control, Throttling & Monetization Rules

> Last Verified: 2026-11-24

## 1. "Inauthentic Content" Policy & Algorithmic Throttling

YouTube updated and expanded its legacy "repetitious content" policy into the **Inauthentic Content Policy**, targeting low-effort mass production while permitting thoughtful AI-assisted workflows:
- **Demonetization & Throttling Triggers**:
  - **Generic / Template-Based / Mass-Produced Videos**: Minimal variation across uploads, basic text-to-speech over stock slideshows, or restating common material without original analysis, instruction, or creative vision.
  - **Disturbing or Emotionally Manipulative Loops**: Staged/fake distress or shock-bait loops designed purely for engagement farming.
  - **Synthetic Personas on High-Stakes Topics**: Using realistic AI avatars/deepfakes or synthetic voices to give advice on **healthcare, finance, legal matters, or news/politics**.
- **Shorts & Reused Content Suppression**:
  - Unoriginal, scraped, or mass-reposted Shorts face severe algorithmic impression throttling even before formal demonetization.
  - Clips, compilations, and reaction videos qualify under YouTube Partner Program (YPP) **only** with substantial transformative value (narrative storytelling, transformative editing, deep commentary).
- **Human-in-the-Loop Reviews**: Ad-suitability checks can delay monetization approval up to 24 hours or re-evaluate existing videos.

## 2. Synthetic & Altered Content Disclosure

- **Disclosure Requirement**: Creators must toggle YouTube's **content disclosure setting** when uploading **realistic** altered or synthetic media (e.g., making a real person appear to say/do something they didn't, altering footage of a real event, or generating realistic scenes).
- **Impact**: Properly disclosing synthetic media does **not** automatically throttle reach or block YPP monetization (unless the content violates core safety/inauthentic policies). Failing to disclose required synthetic media risks removal, monetization suspension, or channel penalties.

## 3. YouTube Shorts External Link Restrictions

- **Clickable Links Disabled**: YouTube disabled **clickable links in YouTube Shorts descriptions, Shorts comments, and the vertical live feed** to curb phishing and spam.
- **Compliant Traffic Routing**: Use clickable links on the **channel profile page** (near Subscribe) and YouTube's native **Related Video** link connecting a Short to a long-form video.

## 4. Community Guidelines Warnings, 90-Day Training & Profanity Rules

- **First-Time Warning + 90-Day Training Expiration**:
  - First-time minor Community Guidelines violations typically trigger a **one-time warning** instead of an immediate strike.
  - Completing the optional in-product **policy training course** causes the warning to **expire 90 days after completion**, provided the creator does not violate that same policy again during the 90-day window (a repeat violation of the same policy within 90 days escalates to a strike).
- **Profanity & Metadata Rules**:
  - Heavy or early profanity (especially in the first seconds of a video, or inside titles, thumbnails, and descriptions) triggers limited/no ads (yellow icon), age restrictions, or removal under Vulgar Language / Harassment guidelines.
- **Spam & Engagement Manipulation**: Engagement pods, artificial views/subs, repetitive comment spam, and deceptive off-platform redirects risk channel termination.

## 5. Pre-Publish & Triage Checklist

1. Ensure videos add clear human commentary, storytelling, or transformation (not raw templates or TTS slideshows).
2. Avoid realistic AI avatars/voices for sensitive YMYL topics (medical, finance, legal, news).
3. Toggle the **altered/synthetic content** disclosure if realistic AI visuals/voices are used.
4. Keep profanity out of titles, thumbnails, descriptions, and the opening seconds.
5. Never rely on clickable URLs in Shorts descriptions/comments; route Shorts viewers via the **Related Video** link or channel profile links.
